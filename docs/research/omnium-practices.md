# Omnium and series scoring: what other venues do

Research done 2026-Oct-20. Every page below was fetched that day unless marked otherwise.

The question: the program's multi-race standings sum Madisons into a Team Omnium, by points or by place with a 7-5-3-2-1 table, the way one Detroit venue runs its weekend omnium (R-TEAM-1 to R-TEAM-5 in `docs/scoring-rules.md`). An individual omnium was wanted too. This note records how other velodromes, clubs, and series score an omnium or a season, one-night and season-long, and what the rulebooks say. It records only what a page says; inference is marked, and "Unknown" means the page does not say.

Companion files: `usac-track-formats.md` section 7 and `uci-track-formats.md` "Omnium" cover the four-race international omnium article by article.

## What the rulebooks say

USA Cycling Rulebook, version 8•25 (`local/research/USACycling_RuleBook_8_2025.pdf`, text by pdftotext):

- 2B19(e), Omnium Scoring: "refers to the cumulative result for a set of races at a competition where points are awarded for each place earned towards a seperate overall omnium standing. In case of a tie on total points, the tie will be broken in favor of the rider who has: (i) The most first place finishes or, if still tied, the most second place finishes, etc., or if still tied; (ii) The highest placing in the last race, or the race nearest the last race of the omnium in which at least one of the tied riders placed."
- Glossary, OMNIUM: "Riders do not have to participate in each event of the omnium unless specified in the event regulations. Different numbers of points may be given in different events. The scoring scheme shall be specified in the official race announcement."
- 2O3, the four-race Omnium: 40-38-36 for the first three races, 21st and below 1 point, the Points Race's own points add to the carried total, a rider abandoning any event is listed after the last placed rider as DNF, two laps down in the Scratch or Tempo costs 40 points, and a tie goes to "the places in the final sprint of the last event, the Points Race".
- 7C3, Junior Track Omniums (national championships, under 17): four races per age group, "first place receiving 1 point, second place receiving 2 points, and so on", lowest total wins; a DNS "will receive last place points plus one, based on the total number of confirmed omnium entrants or 24 riders, whichever is smaller"; standings include every rider who entered one or more races; ties as 2B19(e).

UCI Part 3, 3.2.250 to 3.2.252bis (read in `uci-track-formats.md`, from the 2026 PDF cited there): the same 40-38-36 table and Points Race carry as USAC 2O3; a rider who does not start or abandons "may not continue in the Omnium" and is classified under 3.3.012; the tie-break is the Points Race's final sprint.

So the rulebooks prescribe the four-race omnium and leave every other omnium to the race announcement, with USAC supplying a default tie-break (countback of firsts, then the last race).

## Common patterns

1. Place points from a short table, summed. Every club scheme scores a race by the rider's place, not by the points scored inside it: 5-3-2-1 (San Diego, NSW country series), 7-5-3-2-1 (Alkek's first two races), 6-5-4-3-2-1 (Western Province, Reading's league), 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 (T-Town), 10-9-8-7-6 (JBMV), 15-12-10 (JBMV Grand Prix). Only the four-race international omnium (USAC 2O3, UCI 3.2.251, Dick Lane, Northbrook, Alkek's International Omnium) and SCRL's league add a Points Race's raw points. San Diego says outright that omnium points "may not equal the number of intra-race points obtained".
2. The last race is the heaviest or the tie-breaker. Alkek's third race pays 10-7-5-3-2-1 against 7-5-3-2-1; SCRL doubles its championship race; the international omnium's Points Race is scored raw so it decides the night. Where a tie rule exists, it is the last race (San Diego, Alkek, Western Province, Kissena's forum, USAC 2O3 and UCI by the final sprint) or a countback of firsts, then seconds, then the last race (USAC 2B19(e), JBMV Grand Prix, the NSW series after carnivals attended). No club page uses a head-to-head or a time.
3. A one-night omnium wants every race ridden. San Diego forfeits the night's points for a missed race; Reading's omnium table leaves out riders who did not finish every event; USAC 2O3 and UCI list an abandon as DNF; Northbrook's club version instead deducts 40 points and lets the rider go on. USAC's glossary leaves it to the announcement: "Riders do not have to participate in each event of the omnium unless specified".
4. A season just adds up, and everything counts unless a page says otherwise. T-Town's table sums every date; the NSW series adds "the total points earned at each carnival"; San Diego's Racer of the Year is "the most Omnium Points over the course of the season"; Burnaby excluded only exhibitions. The only drop rules found are Major Taylor's (lowest six races) and the unverified Kissena snippet (worst one). Attendance is rewarded by points, not required by a minimum: the NSW participation points, Western Province's attendance point, San Diego's Iron Racer, Major Taylor's points for every starter.
5. DNS and DNF score nothing, and that is usually all. JBMV's "ZERO points" is the one explicit club rule; Major Taylor pays every starter who is not disqualified; the NSW series and Western Province are silent. The junior national omnium (USAC 7C3) is the exception: a DNS gets last place plus one so a missed race still counts against the rider in a lowest-total scheme.
6. Standings are posted after each night and may change. JBMV updates on Sundays and takes livestream challenges for a week; Major Taylor posts the Friday after each Thursday; the NSW series publishes within a week of each carnival; Reading's 2018 page notes a correction after publication. No page calls a season table "provisional".

## What varies

- Direction. Most club tables are highest-total-wins. The USAC junior national omnium (7C3), the Kissena forum, and a 2010 Forest City championship (search snippet) use lowest-total, one point per place.
- Which races. One-night omniums run from three races (Alkek) through four (international) to every race on the night (San Diego). Seasons exclude named events: exhibitions (Burnaby), Grand Prix weekends (Major Taylor), USAC-sanctioned nights (San Diego), the Madison (Dick Lane 2021), handicap and wheel races (NSW). Some weight one race: Major Taylor's 2.5 multiplier, SCRL's doubling.
- Tables per category. SCRL pays Senior A to 10th, Senior B to 8th, Senior C to 6th; Reading's league and omnium tables differ; the NSW series skips non-qualifying riders so a place pays the next eligible rider; JBMV's team table is shallower than its individual one.
- Bonus points. Major Taylor adds points by field size; the NSW series adds a rising participation bonus; Western Province gives a point for starting.
- Drops. Only Major Taylor (six) and the unverified Kissena page (one). Everyone else sums it all.
- DNS in a one-night omnium. Forfeit the night (San Diego), DNF the omnium (USAC 2O3, UCI, Reading), or a 40-point deduction and carry on (Northbrook).
- Team omniums. Only JBMV's Grand Prix publishes a team table (two-person teams, 8-6-5-4-3-3-2-2-1-1); Dick Lane's team-of-the-year and the Bloomer Park Madison league have no published table. No page describes a team omnium made of Madisons alone, so R-TEAM-1 remains the venue's own scheme.

The short version: a club omnium is a place table summed over a named set of races, the last race settles ties, everything counts unless the announcement excludes it, and the four-race international omnium is the one scheme where a Points Race's own points go straight into the total.
